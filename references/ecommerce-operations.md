# E-Commerce Operations

## Product Sourcing

### Sourcing Models
- **Dropshipping:** Supplier ships directly to customer. Zero inventory risk. Low margins (10-30%). Good for validation.
- **Wholesale:** Buy in bulk at discount, hold inventory, ship yourself. Better margins (40-60%). Requires capital and storage.
- **Private label:** Source generic product, add your brand. Best margins (50-70%). Requires MOQ (minimum order quantity), usually 100-1000 units.
- **Print on demand:** Custom designs printed per order (t-shirts, mugs, posters). Zero inventory. Low margins. Good for testing designs.
- **Digital products:** Zero inventory, zero fulfillment cost, infinite margin. Templates, courses, software, presets, downloads.
- **Handmade/custom:** High margins, limited scale. Good for premium positioning.

### Supplier Platforms
- **Alibaba/AliExpress:** Chinese manufacturers. Low cost, long shipping. Good for private label.
- **Faire:** Wholesale marketplace for indie brands. US/EU suppliers.
- **Printful/Printify:** Print on demand. Integrates with Shopify/Etsy.
- **Spocket:** US/EU dropshipping suppliers. Faster shipping than AliExpress.
- **Local manufacturers:** Higher cost, faster delivery, "Made in USA" positioning.

### Supplier Evaluation
- Order samples before committing
- Check MOQ, lead time, payment terms, defect rate
- Verify communication quality (slow responses = problems later)
- Start with 2-3 suppliers to avoid single-point dependency

## Platform Selection

| Platform | Best For | Fees | Notes |
|----------|----------|------|-------|
| Shopify | Own store, full control | $39/month + 2.9% + $0.30 | Most popular, huge ecosystem |
| Etsy | Handmade, vintage, unique | $0.20/listing + 6.5% + payment fees | Built-in audience, competitive |
| Amazon FBA | Scale, Prime badge | 15% referral + FBA fees | Massive traffic, expensive, Amazon controls the customer |
| Gumroad | Digital products | 10% flat | Simple, fast setup |
| WooCommerce | WordPress sites | Free plugin + hosting | Full control, more technical |
| Big Cartel | Artists, small shops | Free up to 5 products | Simple, limited features |

### Own Store vs Marketplace
- **Marketplace (Etsy, Amazon):** Built-in traffic, competitive, you do not own the customer relationship
- **Own store (Shopify):** Full control, build your brand, own customer data, but you drive all traffic
- Best approach: start on marketplace for validation, build own store for long-term

## Listing Optimization

### Product Title
- Include primary keyword + key attributes (size, color, material, use case)
- Front-load the most important words
- Example: "Professional Invoice Template for Contractors -- Editable PDF, Instant Download"

### Product Images
- Main image: clean, white background, product fills 85% of frame
- Lifestyle images: product in use, real context
- Infographic images: features, dimensions, what's included
- Minimum 5 images. 7-10 is ideal.
- Mobile-first: images must read on a small screen

### Product Description
- Lead with benefits, not features
- Bullet points for scanability
- Address the top 3 objections
- Include social proof (reviews, "X sold", "as seen in")
- Clear CTA ("Add to cart", "Buy now")

### SEO/Keywords
- Research keywords with tools: Google Keyword Planner (free), Ubersuggest, Jungle Scout (Amazon)
- Use exact-match keywords in title, tags, and description
- Long-tail keywords have lower competition: "invoice template for HVAC contractors" beats "invoice template"
- Update keywords based on search analytics quarterly

## Conversion Rate Optimization

### Benchmarks
- E-commerce average conversion rate: 2-3%
- Good: 3-5%
- Excellent: 5-10%
- Below 1%: something is broken (pricing, trust, UX, or traffic quality)

### Quick Wins
- Add trust badges (SSL, money-back guarantee, secure payment icons)
- Show reviews prominently (star rating near the price)
- Simplify checkout (fewer fields, guest checkout option)
- Add urgency (real scarcity only -- "3 left in stock" if true)
- Free shipping threshold ("Free shipping over $50")
- Mobile optimization (50%+ of e-commerce is mobile)

## Fulfillment

### Self-Fulfillment
- Ship within 24-48 hours of order
- Use calculated shipping rates or offer free shipping (build into price)
- Carriers: USPS (cheapest for small/light), UPS/FedEx (faster, heavier items)
- Packaging: branded packaging increases perceived value
- Tracking: always provide tracking. Customers panic without it.

### Third-Party Fulfillment (3PL)
- ShipBob, ShipMonk, Deliverr (now Shopify Fulfillment)
- Send inventory to their warehouse, they pick/pack/ship
- Cost: $3-7 per order + storage fees
- Worth it above 50-100 orders/month

### Returns
- Clear return policy (increases trust and conversion)
- Standard: 30-day returns, customer pays return shipping
- Track return reasons -- repeated returns for same reason = product problem
- Return rate benchmarks: 5-10% for apparel, 2-5% for other physical goods, <1% for digital

## Metrics

| Metric | Formula | Target |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Conversion rate | Orders / Visitors | 2-5% |
| Average order value | Revenue / Orders | Increase with bundles, upsells |
| Cart abandonment | Abandoned / Initiated checkouts | <70% |
| Return rate | Returns / Orders | <5% (non-apparel) |
| Customer acquisition cost | Ad spend / New customers | < 1/3 of AOV |
| Repeat purchase rate | Repeat buyers / Total buyers | >20% |
