# Revenue Models

## SaaS (Software as a Service)
- Recurring subscription revenue (monthly/annual)
- Key metrics: MRR, ARR, churn rate, expansion revenue, net revenue retention
- Pricing tiers based on features, users, or usage
- Free tier drives adoption, paid tiers capture value
- Target: 5-10% monthly free-to-paid conversion, <5% monthly churn
- Strengths: predictable revenue, high LTV, compounding growth
- Weaknesses: slow initial ramp, high churn risk, support costs

## Marketplace
- Transaction fee on each sale (typically 5-20%)
- Chicken-and-egg problem: need supply and demand simultaneously
- Start one-sided: curate supply manually, then attract demand
- Network effects create defensibility once at scale
- Key metrics: GMV, take rate, liquidity, repeat purchase rate

## Productized Service
- Standardized service at fixed price (not hourly consulting)
- Examples: logo design for $299, SEO audit for $499, monthly bookkeeping for $200
- Scalable through SOPs, templates, and automation
- Key metrics: delivery cost, margin per engagement, capacity utilization
- Can transition to SaaS by automating the service layer

## Digital Products
- One-time purchase: templates, courses, ebooks, tools, datasets
- Near-zero marginal cost after creation
- Distribution through marketplaces (Gumroad, Etsy, Udemy) or direct
- Key metrics: units sold, average order value, refund rate
- Bundle and upsell to increase AOV

## Affiliate / Referral
- Commission on sales referred to other businesses (typically 10-50% for digital, 3-10% for physical)
- Requires audience or traffic first
- Low effort once established, but dependent on partner terms
- Key metrics: click-through rate, conversion rate, commission per sale, EPC (earnings per click)

## Advertising / Sponsorship
- Sell attention (display ads, sponsored content, newsletter sponsorships)
- Requires significant audience first (typically 10k+ monthly users)
- CPM rates vary: $5-50 depending on niche and audience quality
- Key metrics: pageviews, impressions, CPM, fill rate, RPM

## Lead Generation
- Collect qualified leads through free tools, content, or services
- Sell leads to businesses in relevant verticals
- Must have explicit consent and clear disclosure
- Key metrics: cost per lead, lead quality score, conversion rate at buyer, revenue per lead
- High margins, scales with traffic

## Data Licensing
- Anonymized aggregate insights from user behavior
- Market intelligence reports, industry benchmarks, trend data
- Requires volume (thousands of users minimum)
- Must comply with privacy regulations
- Key metrics: data points collected, report subscribers, per-report revenue

## Multi-Revenue Architecture
The strongest businesses layer multiple models:
- SaaS product (primary revenue) + aggregate data insights (secondary) + affiliate partnerships (tertiary)
- Free tool (audience builder) + premium upgrade (primary) + lead gen (secondary) + ads (tertiary)
- Every user should contribute to at least one revenue stream, even on a free plan
