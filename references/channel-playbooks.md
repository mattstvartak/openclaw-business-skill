# Channel Playbooks

## SEO / Organic Search
- **Timeline:** 2-6 months to meaningful traffic. Longest payback but highest LTV.
- **Cost:** Near zero (content creation time)
- **Strategy:** Target long-tail keywords with clear purchase intent. "Best invoicing software for plumbers" beats "invoicing software."
- **Execution:** Publish 2-3 high-quality comparison/how-to articles per week. Each article targets one keyword cluster. Include clear CTA.
- **Measurement:** Organic impressions, click-through rate, keyword rankings, organic conversions
- **Best for:** SaaS, digital products, info businesses. Any product with a search-based buyer journey.

## Content Marketing (non-SEO)
- **Timeline:** 1-3 months to build audience
- **Cost:** Creation time, possibly design tools ($0-20/month)
- **Strategy:** Create content that solves the audience's problem, not content about your product. Earn trust, then convert.
- **Formats:** Blog posts, guides, checklists, templates, calculators, free tools
- **Distribution:** Share on social, communities, newsletters. Repurpose across formats.
- **Best for:** Building authority and email lists. Works for any business.

## Social Media (Organic)
- **Timeline:** 1-4 weeks to first traction
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms by audience:**
  - Tradespeople/SMB: Facebook groups, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram
  - B2B/SaaS: LinkedIn, Twitter/X
  - Consumers: TikTok, Instagram, Reddit
  - Developers: Twitter/X, Reddit, Hacker News
- **Strategy:** Provide value in communities first. Answer questions. Share insights. Build reputation. THEN introduce your product when relevant. Never lead with a pitch.
- **Cadence:** Post daily on primary platform. Engage 15-30 min/day in communities.
- **Measurement:** Followers, engagement rate, referral traffic, conversions from social

## Paid Social (Facebook/Instagram/TikTok Ads)
- **Timeline:** Results in 1-3 days
- **Cost:** $5-50/day minimum for testing
- **Strategy:** Test 3-5 ad variations (different hooks, images, CTAs). Kill losers after 48 hours, scale winners.
- **Targeting:** Start broad, let the algorithm find your audience. Narrow only after data shows who converts.
- **Creative:** UGC-style outperforms polished production. Show real use cases, real results, real people.
- **Measurement:** CPM, CPC, CTR, CPA, ROAS
- **Best for:** Products with clear visual value prop and broad appeal

## Google Ads (Search)
- **Timeline:** Results in 1-7 days
- **Cost:** $10-100/day depending on competition
- **Strategy:** Target high-intent keywords only ("buy X", "X pricing", "X vs Y"). Negative match broad terms.
- **Best for:** Products where people actively search for a solution

## Email Marketing
- **Timeline:** 1-2 weeks to set up, ongoing value
- **Cost:** Free up to 1k-10k subscribers on most platforms
- **Strategy:** Capture emails with lead magnets (free tool, template, guide). Nurture with value emails (80% value, 20% pitch). Segment by behavior.
- **Cadence:** 1-2 emails per week. More than 3 = unsubscribes.
- **Measurement:** Open rate (target 30%+), click rate (target 3%+), conversion rate, unsubscribe rate
- **Best for:** Any business. Email is owned audience -- not subject to algorithm changes.

## Cold Outreach (Email/LinkedIn)
- **Timeline:** Results in 1-2 weeks
- **Cost:** $30-100/month for tools (email warmup, LinkedIn automation)
- **Strategy:** Personalized, value-first outreach. Lead with insight, not pitch. "I noticed X about your business -- here is how to fix it" beats "Buy my product."
- **Volume:** 50-100 personalized emails/day. Quality over quantity.
- **Measurement:** Reply rate (target 5-15%), meeting rate, conversion rate
- **Best for:** B2B, high-ticket services, partnerships

## Communities (Reddit, Facebook Groups, Forums, Discord)
- **Timeline:** 1-4 weeks of contribution before promoting
- **Cost:** Free
- **Strategy:** Become a known, helpful member. Answer questions. Share expertise. When someone has a problem your product solves, mention it naturally. NEVER spam.
- **Risk:** Getting banned for self-promotion. Read and follow each community's rules.
- **Best for:** Niche products where the audience congregates in specific communities

## Product Hunt / Launch Platforms
- **Timeline:** Single-day spike, some sustained traffic
- **Cost:** Free
- **Strategy:** Prepare assets, line up supporters, launch on Tuesday-Thursday. Follow up with all who engage.
- **Best for:** SaaS, developer tools, consumer apps. One-time boost, not sustained channel.

## Partnerships / Cross-Promotion
- **Timeline:** 2-4 weeks to establish
- **Cost:** Revenue share or reciprocal promotion
- **Strategy:** Find complementary (non-competing) businesses with the same audience. Propose mutual value.
- **Best for:** Businesses with established audiences looking to expand reach

## Channel Prioritization
On a micro budget, start with:
1. Communities (free, fast feedback)
2. Organic social (free, builds over time)
3. Email capture + nurture (free, owned audience)
4. SEO content (free, compounds)
5. Paid ads (only after organic proves the message works)
