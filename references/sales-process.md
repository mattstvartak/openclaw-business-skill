# Sales Process

## Pipeline Stages

### Standard Pipeline
1. **Lead** -- Someone who matches your target profile but has not engaged
2. **Prospect** -- Lead who has shown interest (visited site, opened email, replied)
3. **Qualified** -- Confirmed need, budget, and authority to buy
4. **Proposal** -- Sent pricing or demo, waiting for decision
5. **Negotiation** -- Discussing terms, handling objections
6. **Closed Won** -- Payment received, deal done
7. **Closed Lost** -- Deal did not happen (track the reason)

### Self-Serve Pipeline (SaaS/Digital Products)
1. **Visitor** -- Landed on site
2. **Signup** -- Created free account or trial
3. **Activated** -- Used the core feature at least once
4. **Engaged** -- Returned 3+ times or hit usage threshold
5. **Converted** -- Upgraded to paid
6. **Retained** -- Still paying after 30+ days

Track conversion rates between each stage. The biggest drop-off is where to focus.

## Outreach

### Cold Email Framework
```
Subject: [Specific observation about their business]

Hi [Name],

[One sentence showing you understand their situation -- reference something specific about them, not generic.]

[One sentence about the problem you solve -- framed as their problem, not your product.]

[One sentence with a specific result or proof point -- number, case study, comparison.]

Would it make sense to [specific low-commitment next step]?

[Your name]
```

- Keep under 100 words
- No attachments on first email
- Personalize the first line (reference their website, recent post, company milestone)
- Send Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM in their timezone
- Follow up 3 times: Day 3, Day 7, Day 14. Then stop.

### Follow-Up Cadence
| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 0 | Initial outreach |
| 3 | Follow-up 1: add new value (article, insight, case study) |
| 7 | Follow-up 2: different angle or question |
| 14 | Follow-up 3: breakup email ("Is this not a priority right now?") |
| 30 | Re-engage only if new trigger event |

### LinkedIn Outreach
- Connect with a personalized note (not a pitch)
- Engage with their content for 1-2 weeks first
- DM with value, not a pitch: share a relevant article, insight, or introduction
- Ask a question, not for a meeting
- Move to email or call only after they respond positively

## Objection Handling

### Common Objections and Responses

**"Too expensive"**
- "What are you comparing it to?" (understand their reference point)
- "What does it cost you to NOT solve this?" (reframe the cost)
- "We have a [cheaper tier / payment plan / annual discount]"

**"I need to think about it"**
- "Totally fair. What specifically do you want to think over?" (surface the real objection)
- "Would it help if I sent a comparison with [competitor]?"
- Set a specific follow-up date

**"We already use [competitor]"**
- "How is that working for you?" (find the pain)
- "Most of our customers switched from [competitor] because of [specific advantage]"
- Do NOT trash the competitor. Differentiate on specific capabilities.

**"Not the right time"**
- "When would be better? I will follow up then." (get a date)
- "Is there a trigger that would make this urgent?" (plant a seed)

**"I need to check with [someone else]"**
- "Would it be helpful if I put together a summary they can review?"
- "Would it make sense to include them in our next conversation?"
- Offer a brief deck or one-pager they can forward

### The Real Objection
80% of stated objections are not the real objection. The real one is usually:
- They do not trust you yet
- They do not understand the value
- They are afraid of change
- They genuinely do not need it (respect this and move on)

Ask questions to find the real blocker. Do not argue with the stated objection.

## Closing

### Closing Techniques
- **Assumptive close:** "Should I set up your account for the Pro plan?" (assume the sale)
- **Alternative close:** "Would you prefer monthly or annual billing?" (not if, but which)
- **Urgency close:** "Our current pricing is locked until [date]" (only if true -- never fabricate urgency)
- **Summary close:** Recap all the value points, then ask for the decision
- **Trial close:** "If we could solve [specific problem], would you move forward?"

### After the Close
- Send a welcome email within 1 hour
- Set up onboarding immediately
- Check in at day 3, day 7, day 30
- Ask for a review/testimonial at day 30 if they are happy
- Ask for a referral at day 60

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Formula |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Reply rate (cold email) | 5-15% | Replies / Emails sent |
| Meeting rate | 2-5% | Meetings / Emails sent |
| Close rate | 20-40% | Deals won / Proposals sent |
| Sales cycle | 7-30 days (SMB) | First contact to payment |
| Average deal size | Varies | Total revenue / Deals closed |
| Pipeline coverage | 3-4x | Pipeline value / Revenue target |

## CRM Basics
- Track every interaction (email, call, meeting) for every prospect
- Set follow-up reminders -- never rely on memory
- Tag leads by source so you know which channels produce
- Review pipeline weekly: move stale deals forward or kill them
- Tools: HubSpot (free CRM), Pipedrive, or even a spreadsheet to start
