# Content Strategy

## Content Types by Purpose

### Acquisition Content (brings new people in)
- **SEO articles:** Target keywords your audience searches. "How to estimate a plumbing job" not "our product features."
- **Comparison posts:** "X vs Y" -- captures people evaluating options. "Jobber vs Housecall Pro" ranks for decision-stage searches.
- **Templates and tools:** Free calculators, checklists, templates. High value, high shareability, captures emails.
- **Video tutorials:** YouTube is the second largest search engine. How-to videos rank and build trust.

### Engagement Content (keeps people coming back)
- **Email newsletters:** Weekly value (tips, insights, trends) with occasional promotion. 80/20 rule.
- **Social media posts:** Quick tips, behind-the-scenes, polls, questions. Platform-native format.
- **Case studies:** Real stories with real numbers. "How [customer] increased revenue 40% in 3 months."

### Conversion Content (turns visitors into customers)
- **Landing pages:** One offer, one CTA, zero distractions. Every word earns its place.
- **Product demos:** Show the product solving the problem. Screen recordings, walkthroughs.
- **Testimonials/reviews:** Social proof at the point of decision. Video testimonials outperform text.
- **Pricing pages:** Clear tiers, feature comparison, FAQ addressing objections.

## SEO Content Clusters

### Structure
```
Pillar Page (broad topic, 2000-3000 words)
  |-- Cluster Article 1 (specific subtopic, 1000-1500 words)
  |-- Cluster Article 2 (specific subtopic, 1000-1500 words)
  |-- Cluster Article 3 (specific subtopic, 1000-1500 words)
  |-- Cluster Article 4 (specific subtopic, 1000-1500 words)
```
All cluster articles link back to pillar. Pillar links to all clusters. This signals topical authority to Google.

### Example Cluster (Trades Business)
- **Pillar:** "Complete Guide to Running a Plumbing Business"
- **Clusters:**
  - "How to Price Plumbing Jobs (With Calculator)"
  - "Best Invoicing Software for Plumbers in 2026"
  - "How to Get More Plumbing Leads Without Ads"
  - "Plumbing Business Insurance: What You Need"
  - "How to Hire Your First Plumbing Apprentice"

### Keyword Research Process
1. Start with seed keywords (what would your customer search?)
2. Expand with Google autocomplete, "People also ask", related searches
3. Check volume and competition (Ubersuggest, Ahrefs free tools, Google Keyword Planner)
4. Prioritize: high intent + low competition + reasonable volume
5. One primary keyword per article. 2-3 secondary keywords.

## Editorial Calendar

### Cadence by Stage
- **Launch (month 1-2):** 3-4 articles/week. Build foundational content fast.
- **Growth (month 3-6):** 2-3 articles/week. Fill gaps, update existing.
- **Maintenance (month 6+):** 1-2 articles/week. Focus on updating and promoting existing content.

### Planning
- Plan 4 weeks ahead minimum
- Mix content types: 50% SEO, 25% social/engagement, 25% conversion
- Tie to product launches, seasonal trends, industry events
- Track what performs and create more of it

## Content Repurposing

One piece of content should become 5-10 pieces across platforms:

```
Long-form blog post (1500 words)
  -> Twitter/X thread (key points)
  -> LinkedIn post (professional angle)
  -> Instagram carousel (visual tips)
  -> TikTok/Reel (60-second version)
  -> Email newsletter section
  -> YouTube video (expanded with examples)
  -> Podcast talking points
  -> Quora/Reddit answers (address specific questions)
  -> Infographic (for Pinterest/sharing)
```

## Video Strategy

### YouTube
- **What works:** Tutorials, how-tos, comparisons, behind-the-scenes, case studies
- **Title:** Include keyword, promise a result. "How to Send Professional Invoices (Free Template)"
- **Thumbnail:** High contrast, large text (3-4 words), human face if possible
- **Length:** 8-15 minutes for SEO. Under 60 seconds for Shorts.
- **Cadence:** 1-2 videos/week minimum to build momentum
- **CTA:** Link to product in description, mention at natural point (not forced)

### TikTok / Reels / Shorts
- **What works:** Quick tips, transformations, relatable pain points, humor
- **Hook:** First 2 seconds must stop the scroll. Lead with the payoff.
- **Length:** 15-60 seconds. Shorter is better for new accounts.
- **Text overlay:** Most viewers watch without sound
- **Cadence:** 1-3 per day during growth phase
- **Hashtags:** 3-5 relevant, mix broad and niche

## Measurement

| Metric | Tool | Target |
|--------|------|--------|
| Organic traffic | Google Search Console / Analytics | Growth month over month |
| Keyword rankings | Ubersuggest / Ahrefs | Top 10 for target keywords |
| Time on page | Analytics | > 2 minutes for articles |
| Email signups | ESP dashboard | 2-5% of visitors |
| Social engagement | Platform analytics | > 3% engagement rate |
| Content -> conversion | UTM tracking | Track which content drives signups |

## Content Quality Checklist

Before publishing, every piece should:
- [ ] Answer a specific question the target audience has
- [ ] Include a clear CTA (email signup, product link, next article)
- [ ] Be scannable (headings, bullets, short paragraphs)
- [ ] Include at least one visual (image, chart, screenshot)
- [ ] Be better than the top 3 results currently ranking for the target keyword
- [ ] Be proofread and factually accurate
- [ ] Have meta title and description optimized for CTR
