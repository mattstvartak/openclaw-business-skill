# Legal Compliance

## Business Basics

### Structure
- Sole proprietorship: simplest, no separation from personal liability
- LLC: liability protection, pass-through taxation, recommended for most small operations
- Corporation: complex, needed for raising investment or issuing stock
- For AI-operated businesses: the human partner should hold the LLC. The AI operates on their behalf.

### Tax Obligations
- Track all revenue and expenses from day one
- Quarterly estimated tax payments if expecting > $1,000 in annual tax liability
- Self-employment tax (~15.3%) on net profit
- Deductible expenses: tools, hosting, domains, APIs, advertising, software subscriptions

## Data Privacy

### CCPA/CPRA (California -- applies if you have California users)
- Right to know what data is collected and how it is used
- Right to delete personal data
- Right to opt out of data sales
- Must disclose data collection in privacy policy
- Applies to businesses with > $25M revenue, 100k+ consumers, or 50%+ revenue from selling data

### GDPR (EU -- applies if you have EU users)
- Explicit consent required before collecting data
- Right to access, correct, and delete personal data
- Data Processing Agreement required with third-party processors
- Privacy by design -- minimize data collection to what is necessary
- 72-hour breach notification requirement

### CAN-SPAM (email)
- Must include physical mailing address in every commercial email
- Must include unsubscribe link
- Must honor unsubscribe within 10 business days
- Subject lines must not be misleading
- "From" address must be accurate

### FTC Guidelines
- Endorsements and testimonials must be honest and not misleading
- Must disclose material connections (paid reviews, affiliate relationships)
- "Results not typical" disclaimer if showing exceptional outcomes
- AI-generated content used in marketing should be disclosed if it could mislead

## Platform Compliance

- Read and follow the terms of service of every platform you use
- Social media: no fake accounts, no engagement manipulation, no spam
- Ad platforms: follow ad policies (no prohibited content, no misleading claims)
- Marketplaces: follow listing guidelines, no review manipulation
- Payment processors: follow acceptable use policies, no prohibited business types

## Intellectual Property

- Do not copy others' content, code, images, or designs without permission
- Stock photos/icons require proper licensing (free options: Unsplash, Pexels, Heroicons)
- Open source code: respect license terms (MIT, Apache = commercial OK; GPL = copyleft obligations)
- Trademarks: do not use competitor brand names in ways that imply endorsement or affiliation
- Your own IP: consider registering trademarks for brand names that gain traction

## AI Transparency

- Never represent AI output as human-created if asked directly
- Marketing materials should not claim "hand-crafted" or "personally written" if AI-generated
- When interacting with customers: "Finch, powered by AI" or "AI business assistant"
- Some platforms and jurisdictions are developing AI disclosure requirements -- monitor changes

## Data Monetization Compliance

- Individual personal data: do NOT sell. Period.
- Anonymized aggregate data: legal with proper consent and anonymization
- Privacy policy must explicitly state that anonymized usage data may be used for market research and insights
- Users must have ability to opt out of data collection beyond what is necessary for the service
- Anonymization must be genuine -- data must not be re-identifiable
- Lead generation: requires explicit consent from the lead before sharing with third parties
