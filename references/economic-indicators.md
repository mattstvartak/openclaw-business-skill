# Economic Indicators

## What to Monitor

### Consumer Health
| Indicator | Source | Frequency | What It Tells You |
|-----------|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| Consumer Confidence Index | Conference Board | Monthly | Willingness to spend |
| Consumer Sentiment | U. Michigan | Monthly | Spending outlook |
| Personal Savings Rate | BEA/FRED | Monthly | How much buffer consumers have |
| Credit Card Delinquency Rate | Fed | Quarterly | Financial stress level |
| Retail Sales | Census Bureau | Monthly | Actual consumer spending |

### Business Environment
| Indicator | Source | Frequency | What It Tells You |
|-----------|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| GDP Growth Rate | BEA | Quarterly | Overall economic direction |
| Inflation (CPI) | BLS | Monthly | Price pressure on consumers and costs |
| Federal Funds Rate | Federal Reserve | 8x/year | Cost of borrowing, affects all spending |
| Unemployment Rate | BLS | Monthly | Labor market health, side-income demand |
| PMI (Purchasing Managers Index) | ISM | Monthly | Business activity and outlook |
| Small Business Optimism | NFIB | Monthly | SMB willingness to invest |

### Tech/SaaS Specific
| Indicator | Source | Frequency | What It Tells You |
|-----------|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| SaaS Magic Number | Public SaaS metrics | Quarterly | Market health for subscription businesses |
| VC Funding Volume | Crunchbase/PitchBook | Quarterly | Competition intensity, market exuberance |
| Layoff Tracker | Layoffs.fyi | Weekly | Budget cuts = churn risk + demand for automation |
| OpenRouter/AI API pricing | OpenRouter | Ongoing | Your operating cost trajectory |

## How to Interpret

### Recession Signals
When 2+ of these are true, consumer spending is tightening:
- Consumer confidence declining 3+ months
- Unemployment rising
- Fed raising rates
- Retail sales declining
- Credit delinquencies rising

**Response:** Price aggressively. Position as cost-saver. Lean into "this pays for itself" messaging. Offer generous free tiers. Cut nice-to-have features, double down on must-have.

### Growth Signals
When 2+ of these are true, consumers are spending:
- Consumer confidence rising
- Unemployment low/declining
- GDP growing 2%+
- Retail sales rising
- Small business optimism high

**Response:** Test premium pricing. Launch higher tiers. Invest in brand. People buy convenience and quality when they feel wealthy.

### Counter-Cyclical Opportunities
Downturns create specific demand:
- Budget alternatives to expensive incumbents (your pricing advantage)
- Automation tools that replace headcount
- Side-income and freelance tools (unemployment drives entrepreneurship)
- DIY tools (people do things themselves instead of hiring)
- Skill-building and education (people invest in themselves during downturns)

### Where to Find Data
- **FRED (Federal Reserve Economic Data):** fred.stlouisfed.org -- free, comprehensive, API available
- **BLS (Bureau of Labor Statistics):** bls.gov -- employment, inflation, wages
- **Census Bureau:** census.gov -- retail sales, business formation
- **Conference Board:** conference-board.org -- consumer confidence
- **Google Trends:** trends.google.com -- real-time search interest by topic
- **Reddit/Twitter/X:** Real-time sentiment (filter for signal, ignore noise)
